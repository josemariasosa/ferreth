### Production integration and GREEN

The strict detailed decoder admits reachable GAS with its byte offset and projects it to `Instruction::Gas`. `Opcode` still identifies standalone ADD/SUB leaves. `ProgramRunner` rejects GAS with a guest-path error. The historical compiler oracle rejects it as outside its supported subset, and the historical research and arithmetic test consumers reject it explicitly. The canonical compiler adds the occurrence-specific `guest_gas_<index>` entry and successor, local charge and overflow branch destinations, and shared overflow and OOG publisher declarations. [Decoder](../../crates/ferreth-compiler/src/lib.rs) · [Symbolic compiler](../../crates/ferreth-compiler/src/compiler/symbolic.rs) · [GAS emitter](../../crates/ferreth-compiler/src/rv64/opcodes/gas.rs)

The shared emitter charges 2 with unsigned sufficiency, subtracts once, and stores live gas at metadata offset 32. On failure it jumps to OOG before debit or stack access. The capacity guard overwrites `t0` and preserves the metadata base in `t1`. Funded overflow transfers to the shared overflow publisher before any stack effects. The success path reloads post-charge gas from `t1`, writes four little-endian limbs, advances `s1` by 32 once, then jumps to the next guest occurrence, STOP, or normal publication at EOF. The emitter uses ABI constants for stack size, word size and live gas offset. The existing publisher and sentinel handle zero settlement and status-last publication. PUSH1 retains its emission and verifier behavior. The existing guest ABI and canonical execute entry point are sufficient.

## Phase 4C — Hardening

### Final-image contracts and corruption controls

Independent verification checks every word of GAS. Words 0–7 check metadata address and load, cost, unsigned branch (4 → 6), OOG JAL (5), debit and store. Words 8–10 compute the stack limit and branch unsigned to word 18 on full. Word 11 reloads live gas, words 12–15 store low then three zero limbs, and word 16 advances the pointer. Word 17 must jump to the fixed-layout successor and word 18 to overflow. Exact publisher words include gas and status stores followed by the sentinel JALR. Declaration verification remains a separate check. [Fixed-layout verifier](../../crates/ferreth-compiler/src/rv64/verify.rs)

The focused library command rejected 18 individual GAS body mutations and nine normal/overflow/OOG publisher mutations. The body mutations cover cost, both unsigned guards, debit, live metadata offsets and reload base, missing/diverted exception transfers, shortened stack limit, early store, low and upper limb corruption, wrong pointer advance and bypassed STOP. Four additional reassembled mutations explicitly passed declaration verification with matching corrupted declarations before failing independent verification. They used a signed sufficiency branch, redirected OOG to overflow, redirected overflow to OOG, and skipped STOP.
